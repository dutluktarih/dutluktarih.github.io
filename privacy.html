Privacy Policy – Dutluk Tarih Paylaşım

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Dutluk Tarih Paylaşım is a private tool used only by the owner of the “Buralar Eskiden Dutluktu” (@dutluktarih) accounts on YouTube, Instagram and TikTok to upload the owner’s own videos to those accounts. It is not offered to the public.

What data we access: With the account owner’s permission (OAuth), the tool uploads the owner’s own videos, manages the owner’s own YouTube channel settings and playlists, and reads basic information about the owner’s own accounts (account name and ID) to confirm it is connected to the correct account, plus view and like counts of the owner’s own posts. It does not access any other user’s data.

What we store: Access authorizations (tokens) stored only on the owner’s computer, and the IDs of uploaded videos in a local log file. No personal data of viewers or other users is collected, stored, sold or shared.

Third parties: YouTube Terms of Service https://www.youtube.com/t/terms, Google Privacy Policy https://policies.google.com/privacy, TikTok Terms of Service https://www.tiktok.com/legal/terms-of-service, Meta / Instagram Terms https://help.instagram.com/581066165581870.

Revoking access and deletion: Access can be revoked at any time from Google account permissions, TikTok app settings (Security → Manage app permissions) or Instagram settings (Apps and websites). When access is revoked, the stored authorization is deleted from the owner’s computer; the local upload log can be deleted on request within 7 days.
